YT Automation — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

YT Automation is a personal desktop tool operated by a single individual (the "creator") to prepare short videos and send them to the creator's own social media accounts. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What information the app handles

  • Authorization tokens. When the creator connects his own TikTok account, TikTok provides an access token and a refresh token. They are stored only in a file on the creator's own computer.
  • Videos. The videos the app uploads are created by the creator on his own computer.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect, read, store or share personal data of any other TikTok user.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • It does not send any information to servers other than TikTok's own API.
  • It does not sell or share information with third parties.

How the information is used

The token is used only to upload the creator's own videos to his own TikTok inbox as drafts, through TikTok's Content Posting API. The creator reviews and publishes each draft himself in the TikTok app.

Retention and deletion

The creator can delete the stored tokens at any time by deleting the app's local data, and can revoke the app's access from TikTok's settings (Security & permissions → Manage app permissions).
