YT Automation

A personal desktop tool for preparing short vertical videos.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

What it is

YT Automation is a desktop application used by a single creator to produce short videos for his own channels. It writes a short script from a public source, records a voice-over, assembles the video with real footage and captions, and keeps a calendar of upcoming posts.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit (Desktop): the creator signs in once with his own TikTok account.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload): the finished video is uploaded to the creator's own TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is published automatically: the creator opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft and taps Post himself.

Data

The app only stores the creator's own authorization tokens, locally on his computer. It does not read or store data of other TikTok users. See the Privacy Policy.
